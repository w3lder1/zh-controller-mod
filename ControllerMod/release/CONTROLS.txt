ZERO HOUR CONTROLLER MOD - CONTROLS (Xbox controller, default buttons)
======================================================================

In a match press View (the small left button) for this help on screen.
A, B, X, Y, LB and RB can be moved around in the controller settings
(View in any menu; in a match: View, then Y).

CAMERA
  Left stick ............ move the camera (the crosshair stays in the middle)
  LT (hold) ............. move faster (while aiming or placing: slower)
  RT (hold) ............. while aiming or placing: faster
  Right stick ........... left/right rotate, up/down zoom
  Right stick click ..... reset the camera

SELECTING
  A ..................... select what is under the crosshair
                          (on one of your buildings: opens its command wheel)
  A A (double tap) ...... select all units of that type on screen
  Hold A ................ paint: every unit the circle touches gets selected
  LB .................... whole army        RB ... army on screen
  RT .................... cycle through the unit types in your selection
  B ..................... cancel / deselect

ORDERS
  X ..................... order: move, attack, enter, repair... (what fits)
  X X (double tap) ...... guard that spot
  Hold X and move ....... draw a line: the selection spreads along it
    + left stick click .. while drawing: guard line (blue)
  LB + RB together ...... unload selected transports and buildings

WHEELS (like Halo Wars)
  Y ..................... command wheel of your selection: build, train,
                          upgrades, abilities. D-pad down/up in it: build
                          queue, Orders (force attack, waypoints)
  D-pad up .............. generals powers (D-pad down in it: promotions)
  D-pad down ............ control groups (A: select, Y: assign, X: clear)
  Stick ................. point at a slice (it stays highlighted)
  A or left stick click . use it
  X or LB ............... take one out of the queue (hold: take all)
  B or Y ................ close
  With more than 10 slices there are two wheels: left stick and right stick.

AIMING A POWER / PLACING A BUILDING
  A or X ................ confirm      B ... back
  Right stick ........... turn the building

JUMPS
  D-pad left ............ your bases (press again: the next one)
  D-pad right ........... latest alerts (press again: the one before)

MENUS (main menu, skirmish setup, options, save/load, pause menu)
  D-pad / left stick .... move the gold frame
  A ..................... select; on a drop-down list: open it, then
                          Up/Down to choose, A keep, B cancel
  Left / Right .......... on a slider (volume, game speed): change it
  LB / RB ............... tabs (Official / Unofficial maps)
  Start ................. jump to Play Game
  B ..................... back ("Are you sure?" boxes: No)
  View .................. controller settings
  A, B or Start ......... at start-up: skip the videos
  Menu (in a match) ..... pause menu

BUTTONS MIXED UP?
  Hold both stick clicks for 3 seconds, or press Ctrl+Shift+F12:
  the default buttons come back.

Typing a player name or a save-game name needs the keyboard.
The mouse and keyboard keep working at any time.
